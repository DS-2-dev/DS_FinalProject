      Classifiers (exploratory)

        Caveats

        • Self-reported hours and a convenience sample, mostly ages 16–25.
        • Observational data: these are associations, not causes.
        • One hours answer was imputed with the median.
        • There is no correction for multiple comparisons.

        Method notes

        • Survey: a Google Form. Participants judged 20 images as AI-generated or human-made, then reported their age, gender and weekly hours online.
        • Cleaning: names are replaced by a hash. Hours are parsed from free text (ranges are averaged, per-day answers are multiplied by 7, minutes are converted to hours). One unparseable answer is imputed with the median (21 h).
        • Main model: score ~ hours + gender + age, OLS with HC3 robust standard errors. Within each gender: score ~ hours + age.
        • Classifiers: label = low performance (score ≤ 60%). Evaluated with stratified cross-validation; subgroups need n ≥ 6 and at least 2 of each class.
