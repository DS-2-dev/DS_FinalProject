    Can we predict low scorers?

    Method

    • Google Forms survey. Participants judged 20 images as AI-generated or human-made, then reported their age, gender and weekly hours online.
    • Names were replaced by hashes. One missing hours answer was filled with the median.
    • Main model: linear regression of score on hours, gender and age, with robust (HC3) standard errors. Within each gender: score on hours and age.
    • Limits: a self-reported, mostly 16–25 convenience sample. Associations only, not causes.
